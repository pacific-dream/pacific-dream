Better Run Through the Jungle

"Better Run Through the Jungle" is a 3D first-person shooter powered by Unreal Engine 5.1. The game features intense gunfights, terrifying zombies, and horror-filled interiors, capturing the spirit and aesthetic of 1980s B-class movies.

The Bunker: Nacht der Untoten

In “The Bunker: Nacht der Untoten”, the player plays as a WW2 US Marines soldier who have a special mission in Europe ,1945. To get the intel of the secert weapon of Nazi Germany . But mission failed, player have to get out of the jailed bunker and escape from the hunt of Hitler’s undead Army.

Murder in Bangzijing: Movie Adapted Board Game

In "Murder in Bangzijing: Movie Adapted Board Game," players take on the roles of students trying to escape the infamous haunted dorm, Bangzijing. They must race through the eerie corridors, either striving to be the last person standing or the first to reach the exit, all while evading the dangers lurking within.

Be the Top Fighters

"Be the Top Fighters" is a VR first-person interactive movie game based on a well-received short film series. Players can experience thrilling first-person combat and the harsh realities of war in this immersive experience.

Rakuen : Watasi Gai Nai Rakuen

Players will use the visual difference to solve puzzles and escape from monsters based on the perspective effect of objects being larger when closer and smaller when farther away. The game is set in an abandoned amusement park with a horror atmosphere. The enemies are scary dolls. Because of the visual difference, this game has an absurd and bizarre experience

Lifetakers & Heartbreakers

“Lifetakers & Heartbreakers” is a 2D side-scrolling shooter game where players take on the roles of three elite operators battling the evil PRA forces in the Eastern European country of Pavlovia to thwart a nuclear threat. This game is set within the "Pavlovia" universe.

AIGC & VR

Neo-Tech Production

THUNDERFIRE:

HEART OF DARKNESS

ThunderFire: Heart of Darkness is an experimental short film created to explore a new workflow that integrates live-action filmmaking, AIGC (AI-generated content), and virtual production.

Building on the world of Green Run, the story follows Allied soldier Alan Mitchell, the sole survivor of a failed mission who becomes entangled in a web of betrayal, secret experiments, and internal conspiracies within the United Forces.

Combining real-world cinematography with AI-assisted environments and digital effects, the film serves as both a cinematic narrative and a technical exploration of how emerging AI and virtual production tools can expand creative possibilities in modern filmmaking.

Fukushima Radiation

VR Documentory

I personally ventured alone into the restricted radiation zones of Fukushima, Japan, to document the silent aftermath of the 2011 nuclear disaster. Using an Insta360 camera, I recorded immersive VR footage that captures the eerie coexistence of decay and renewal—collapsed buildings overtaken by nature, remnants of daily life frozen in time, and the invisible weight of radiation that still lingers.

Pavlovia Series

This series primarily focuses on the turmoil and conflicts occurring within and around a fictional former Soviet republic after the collapse of the Soviet Union: Pavlovia. Since the 1990s, this country has been mired in continuous conflict. In the 2020s, to eliminate insurgents entrenched in the demilitarized zone, the U.S. Marine Corps' "Intruder" unit was dispatched to Pavlovia's capital, Zhukovsk, stationed at the Cpt. Charlie Walker Memorial Combat Base to investigate intelligence. This is where the story begins.

Bangzijing Series

The Murder in Bangzijing Thriller Series is a collection of short films produced by Pacific Dream Studio between 2022 and 2024, It includes three films: Murder in Bangzijing (2022), Murder in Bangzijing: Bloody Hell (2023), and Murder in Bangzijing: Twilight Zone (2024). All the stories are set in a dormitory where, upon moving in, students begin to notice eerie and unsettling things. Over time, they discover the building is haunted, while the school attempts to cover up the truth. The students must fight the ghosts and escape.

Pacific Dream Studio

Pacific Dream was established by Hans Lee Richtofen in 2020 and is headquartered at Bangzijing Apartment, Room 1609, Beijing, China. The studio comprises several key creators and a team of over a dozen collaborators. We focus on the creative intersection of film and gaming, specializing in innovative forms that blend the two mediums.
